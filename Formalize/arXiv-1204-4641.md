# arXiv 1204.4641

Title: The ranks of central factor and commutator groups
Authors: Leonid A. Kurdachenko, Pavel Shumyatsky
Year: 2012

### Main results

**Theorem 1.** Let G be a finite group such that G/Z(G) has rank r. Then the rank of G' is r-bounded.

**Theorem 2.** Let G be a locally generalized radical group such that G/Z(G) has finite rank r. Then the rank of G' is finite and r-bounded.

### Supporting results

- Lemma 3: If G = <N, X> with N normal abelian, then [N,G] = prod_{x in X} [N,x].
- Lemma 4: If G/Z(G) has finite rank r and M is a normal abelian subgroup, then [M,G] has rank at most r^2.
- Lemma 5: If G/Z(G) is soluble with derived length d and rank r, then r(G') <= (1/2)dr(r-1) + (d-1)r^2.
- Lemma 6: If G is a finite p-group of exponent dividing p^n and rank at most r, then |G| <= p^s for some s = s(n,r).
- Proposition 7: If G is a finite nilpotent group, then the rank of G' is bounded in terms of r = r(G/Z(G)) only.
- Lemma 8: If G is a finite soluble group of rank r, then the derived length of G/F(G) is r-bounded.
- Lemma 9: If G is a finite group of rank r with no nontrivial normal soluble subgroups, then G has a normal series M <= T <= G with M a direct product of at most r non-abelian simple groups, T/M soluble, and |G/T| <= r!.
- Corollary 10: If G/Z(G) is locally finite of finite rank r, then G' is locally finite and r(G') is r-bounded.
- Lemma 11: If G is a finitely generated generalized radical group of finite rank r with no nontrivial normal periodic subgroups, then G has a subgroup of r-bounded index which is soluble with r-bounded derived length.
