# arXiv 2509.17780

Title: p-Groups with derived length three and three character degrees
Year: 2025

### Theorem 3.3 (Non-existence of high-order automorphisms of extraspecial groups)

Let G be an extraspecial group of order p^(2n+1). For p > 2n there does not exist an automorphism of G of order p^n for n > 1. In particular for n = 5, p = 3 is the only odd prime admitting an automorphism of order p^2. The paper constructs (not classifies) examples of p-groups with exactly three character degrees and derived length 3 as semidirect products extending extraspecial and non-extraspecial groups.
