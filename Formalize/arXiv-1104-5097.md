# arXiv 1104.5097

Title: Search and test algorithms for Triple Product Property triples
Year: 2011

### Hedtke-Murthy Theorem 3.1 (Set-theoretic characterization of TPP)

Three subsets S, T, U of G form a basic TPP triple iff (i) 1 in S cap T cap U, (ii) Q(T) cap Q(U) = {1}, and (iii) Q(S) cap Q(T)Q(U) = {1}. For subgroups (where Q(H) = H) this simplifies to T cap U = {1} and S cap TU = {1}. Since any TPP triple translates to a basic one, this characterization is algorithmically verifiable and is the test used in all GAP/Sage TPP search implementations.

### Hedtke-Murthy Theorem 3.5 (Normal subgroup in subgroup TPP triple)

If (S, T, U) is a subgroup TPP triple and one of them is normal in G, then |S||T||U| <= |G|. This means the search for nontrivial subgroup TPP triples can be restricted to non-normal subgroups, a critical observation for the sieve.

### Hedtke-Murthy Tables 1-4 (Exhaustive TPP capacity data)

Exhaustive computations of beta, beta_g, D_3, beta/D_3, and beta/|G| for all nonabelian groups of order < 25 (Table 1). For larger groups only the subgroup capacity beta_g is computed: selected 2-groups of order 64 and 128 (Table 2), and selected PSL_2(F_q) and SL_n(F_q) groups (Tables 3-4) reporting D_3, beta_g, beta_g/D_3, and beta_g/|G|. These tables are the validation target for the group sieve.
