# arXiv 2311.06666

Title: On the modular isomorphism problem for groups with center of index at most p^3
Year: 2023

### Modular isomorphism problem for small central index

Results on when two p-groups with center of index at most p^3 can be distinguished by their group algebras. Relevant because groups with |G:Z(G)| <= p^3 have rho_0 = 1 by Murthy's theorem, and understanding their fine structure aids the sieve's classification.
