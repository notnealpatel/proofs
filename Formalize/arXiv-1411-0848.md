# arXiv 1411.0848

Title: Commuting probabilities of finite groups
Year: 2014

### Eberhard Conjecture 1.1 (Joseph's conjectures on Pr(G))

Joseph conjectured that: (J1) Every limit point of {Pr(G)} is rational, and (J2) Pr(G) is well-ordered by >. This paper proves both conjectures. Corollary 1.3 shows that all limit points of P are rational and P is well-ordered by >. The commutativity degree spectrum directly constrains which groups the sieve can distinguish.

### Eberhard Corollary 1.3 (Pr(G) is well-ordered)

All limit points of P = {Pr(G) : G finite} are rational, and P is well-ordered by >. This structural result about the commutativity degree spectrum is a foundational fact for classifying groups by Pr(G).

### Eberhard Theorem 2.2 (Neumann's theorem for bilinear maps)

Let phi: A x B -> C be a bilinear map of finite abelian groups with Pr(phi) >= epsilon > 0. Then there are subgroups A' <= A and B' <= B such that |A/A'|, |B/B'|, and |phi(A',B')| are each epsilon-bounded. This is the bilinear-map generalization of Neumann's theorem connecting large Pr(phi) to near-triviality of phi on large subgroups.
