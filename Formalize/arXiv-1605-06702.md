# arXiv 1605.06702

Title: On Cap Sets and the Group-theoretic Approach to Matrix Multiplication
Year: 2016

### Blasiak-Church-Cohn-Grochow-Naslund-Sawin-Umans Theorem B (STPP barrier for abelian groups of bounded exponent)

For every l in N, there exists epsilon_l > 0 such that no STPP construction in any abelian group of exponent at most l is large enough to yield a bound better than omega <= 2 + epsilon_l via the abelian STPP inequality (sum_i (|A_i||B_i||C_i|)^{omega/3} <= |H|). This is the cap set barrier. It rules out the specific abelian-group constructions used for the Coppersmith-Winograd bound but leaves nonabelian groups and abelian groups with large cyclic factors wide open.
