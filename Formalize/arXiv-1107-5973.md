# arXiv 1107.5973

Title: Upgrading Subgroup Triple Product Property Triples
Year: 2011

### Hedtke Lemma 1 (Right quotient growth bound for subgroup extension)

If X is a subgroup of G and c in G \ X, then 2|X| <= |Q(X cup {c})| <= 3|X|. That is, adding a single element to a subgroup at least doubles the right quotient size and at most triples it. This bound is used to determine whether an upgrade step (extending a subgroup TPP triple to a larger subset TPP triple) is feasible.
