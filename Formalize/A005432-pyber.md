CLAIM
  A005432(n) = number of distinct subgroups of S_n
  (conjugates counted separately). Pyber conjectured
    log2 a(n) = (1/16) n^2 + o(n^2).
  Roney-Dougal and Tracey proved the stronger matching
  bounds in 2025.

LEAN
  `Proofs/GroupCount/SubgroupCountSn.lean` defines
  `GroupCount.numSubgroupsSymm` and the exact asymptotic
  predicates. `GroupCount.pyber_conjecture` remains an
  intentional `sorry`; the sorry-free reduction
  `GroupCount.PyberConjecture.of_roneyDougalTracey`
  only proves that the published stronger theorem implies it.

ROUTE
  Formalize the Roney-Dougal–Tracey upper and lower bounds,
  including their finite-group enumeration and asymptotic
  estimates. Publication settles the mathematics, not the
  repository target.

EVIDENCE
  Roney-Dougal–Tracey, Theorem 1, gives
  `2^(n²/16+α n log n) ≤ a(n) ≤ 2^(n²/16+β n^(3/2))`
  for absolute constants `α>0` and `β` and all `n>1`.
