# arXiv 2002.09472

Title: Geometric Rank of Tensors and Subrank of Matrix Multiplication
Year: 2020

### Kopparty-Moshkovitz-Zuiddam (geometric rank of tensors)

Introduces geometric rank as a tool for bounding the subrank and border subrank of tensors. Proves Q(T) <= Q_bar(T) <= GR(T) <= SR(T) and computes GR(<m,m,m>) = ceil(3m^2/4), matching Strassen's lower bound on border subrank exactly.
