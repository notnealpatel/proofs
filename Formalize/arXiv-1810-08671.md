# arXiv 1810.08671

Title: Limits on All Known (and Some Unknown) Approaches to Matrix Multiplication
Year: 2018

### Alman-Vassilevska Williams Theorem 1.1 (Limits on Galactic method for generalized CW tensors)

There is a universal constant l > 2, independent of q, such that for every generalized CW_q tensor T, omega_g(T) >= l. That is, the Galactic method (which subsumes both the laser method and the group-theoretic approach) cannot prove omega = 2 when applied to any generalized Coppersmith-Winograd tensor, even in the limit as q -> infinity.
