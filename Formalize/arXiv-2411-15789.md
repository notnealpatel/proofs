# arXiv 2411.15789

Title: Asymptotic tensor rank is characterized by polynomials
Year: 2024

### Polynomial characterization of asymptotic tensor rank

The asymptotic rank of a tensor is characterized by polynomial equations: for any real number r, the sublevel set {T : asymp_rank(T) <= r} is Zariski-closed, i.e., determined by the vanishing of finitely many polynomials on the tensor space. This connects to the matrix multiplication exponent because 2^omega = asymp_rank(<2,2,2>).
