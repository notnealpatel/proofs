# arXiv 2307.07970

Title: New Bounds for Matrix Multiplication: from Alpha to Omega
Year: 2023

### Vassilevska Williams-Xu-Xu-Zhou (omega <= 2.371552)

Further improvements to the omega bound via an improved variant of the laser method, building on Duan-Wu-Zhou (FOCS 2023). Also improves the dual exponent alpha >= 0.321334. The latest records inform what the sieve needs to beat.
