# arXiv 2112.08681

Title: On the commuting probability of p-elements in a finite group
Year: 2021

### Theorem A (Normal abelian Sylow characterization)

Let G be a finite group and let p be a prime. Then Pr_p(G) > f(p) = (p^2+p-1)/p^3 if and only if G has a normal and abelian Sylow p-subgroup.

### Theorem B (Simple group equality case)

Let G be a nonabelian finite simple group and let p be a prime divisor of |G|. Then Pr_p(G) = f(p) if and only if p >= 5 and G is isomorphic to PSL_2(p).

### Theorem C (Centralizer ratio bound)

Let G be a finite group and let p be a prime. Then |C_G(x)_p| / |G_p| <= 1/p for every p-element x in G \ O_p(G). This bounds the proportion of p-elements centralizing a fixed p-element outside the largest normal p-subgroup, and is a key ingredient in the proof of Theorem A.
