# arXiv 1702.00905

Title: Bounds for Matchings in Nonabelian Groups
Year: 2017

### Sawin Theorem 1.5 (Matching bounds in nonabelian groups)

Bounds on the size of multiplicative matchings in nonabelian groups, generalizing cap set bounds. Let G be a nontrivial finite group. There exists a constant delta<1 such that any multiplicative matching in G^n has size at most delta^n |G|^n. This connects combinatorial extremal problems in groups to the MM exponent via the simultaneous triple product property, and constrains what the group-theoretic approach can achieve with specific group families.
