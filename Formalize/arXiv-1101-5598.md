# arXiv 1101.5598

Title: A note on the group-theoretic approach to fast matrix multiplication
Author: Ivo Hedtke
Year: 2011

### Hedtke Theorem 1 (Identity and disjointness normalization of TPP triples)

If S', T' and U' fulfill the TPP, then there exists a triple S, T and U with |S|=|S'|, |T|=|T'|, |U|=|U'| and S ∩ T = T ∩ U = S ∩ U = {1} which also fulfills the TPP. That is, any TPP triple can be assumed to contain the identity and be otherwise disjoint without loss of generality.

### Hedtke Theorem 4 (Murthy's minimal disjointness property, shorter proof)

If S, T and U fulfill the TPP then |X ∩ Y| <= 1 holds for all X != Y in {S, T, U}. Originally due to Murthy (2009); Hedtke gives a shorter proof via right quotient intersection properties.

### Hedtke Theorem 5 (Right quotient additive bound)

If S, T and U fulfill the TPP then |Q(S)| + |Q(T)| + |Q(U)| <= |G| + 2. This is stronger than Corollary 6 since |X| <= |Q(X)| always holds.

### Hedtke Corollary 6 (Murthy's additive bound, shorter proof)

If S, T and U fulfill the TPP then |S| + |T| + |U| <= |G| + 2. Originally due to Murthy (2009); Hedtke gives a much shorter proof via right quotient set intersections and inclusion-exclusion.
