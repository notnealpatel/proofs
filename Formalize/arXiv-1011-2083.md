# arXiv 1011.2083

Title: Central quotient versus commutator subgroup of groups
Year: 2010

### Yadav Theorem A (Upper bound |G/Z(G)| <= product of conjugacy class sizes)

If G/Z(G) is finitely generated by x_1 Z(G), ..., x_t Z(G) with finite conjugacy classes, then |G/Z(G)| <= product_{i=1}^t |x_i^G|. This is a strengthened Schur-type bound on central index. Formalizing this connects the sieve's |G:Z(G)| invariant to conjugacy class structure.

### Yadav Corollary after Proposition 3.5 + Theorem 3.8 (Class 2 p-groups with Property A: classification up to isoclinism)

For a finite p-group G of class 2 satisfying |G/Z(G)| = |gamma_2(G)|^d (Property A), the commutator subgroup gamma_2(G) is cyclic and G/Z(G) is homocyclic (Corollary after Proposition 3.5). Moreover, G is isoclinic to a central product Y = G_1 *_X G_2 *_X ... *_X G_m of 2-generated p-groups where each factor satisfies gamma_2(G_i) = Z(G_i) is cyclic of order q = p^n (Theorem 3.8). This structural classification is relevant because class-2 p-groups are a major focus of the sieve.
