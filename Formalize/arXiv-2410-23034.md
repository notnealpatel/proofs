# arXiv 2410.23034

Title: The Conjugacy Ratio of abelian-by-cyclic groups
Year: 2024

### Theorem 1.2 (Conjugacy ratio decay for exponential growth)

Let G = K semidirect <t> be a finitely generated abelian-by-cyclic group with exponential growth, with generating set S of the standard form. Then c(S^r)/|S^r| = O(log r / r). This quantitative bound on the conjugacy ratio implies it tends to zero, confirming the Ciobanu-Cox-Martino conjecture for these groups and generating sets.

### Corollary 1.3 (Conjugacy ratio classification)

Let G = K semidirect <t> be a finitely generated abelian-by-cyclic group with generating set S of the standard form. Then lim c(S^r)/|S^r| = 0 if and only if G is not virtually abelian. Combines Theorem 1.2 (exponential growth case) with prior work of Ciobanu-Cox-Martino on the polynomial growth case.
