# arXiv 1706.05074

Title: Polynomials and the exponent of matrix multiplication
Year: 2017

### Polynomial characterization of omega

Characterizes the MM exponent omega as the liminf of log_n of the tensor rank, border rank, Waring rank, or symmetric border rank of any of four polynomial formulations of the matrix multiplication tensor: the symmetrized tensor sM_n = trace(A^3), its restriction to symmetric matrices (sM_n^S), to traceless symmetric matrices (sM_n^{S,0}), and to symmetric matrices with zero diagonal (sM_n^Z).
