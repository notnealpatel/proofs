# arXiv 1712.02302

Title: Which groups are amenable to proving exponent two for matrix multiplication?
Year: 2017

### BCCGU Theorem 3.19 (Nilpotent groups of bounded exponent cannot prove omega = 2)

STPP constructions in families of nilpotent groups of bounded exponent cannot achieve omega = 2 if they have either bounded variance or linear expectation of their p-degrees. The proof generalizes the polynomial method from the cap set resolution, showing the shrinkage rate of powers of the augmentation ideal mirrors that of low-degree polynomials over (Z/pZ)^n.

### BCCGU Corollary 3.9 (Bounded length p-lower central series p-groups cannot prove omega = 2)

STPP constructions in families of p-groups of bounded exponent and bounded length of p-lower central series cannot achieve omega = 2. This follows from Theorem 3.8 (the p-groups main theorem) since bounded length of the p-lower central series implies bounded variance.

### BCCGU Theorem 4.2 (Symmetric groups with Young subgroups cannot prove nontrivial omega)

Let H_1, H_2, H_3 be Young subgroups of S_n with pairwise trivial intersections. Then |S_n| / (|H_1| |H_2| |H_3|)^{2/3} >= e^{cn - d sqrt(n) log n} for universal constants c, d > 0. This exponential gap rules out Young subgroups as a route to nontrivial omega bounds.

### BCCGU Corollary 2.11 (Key corollary: slice rank barrier for STPP)

Given a family of groups G with slicerank(M_G) <= |G|^{1 - Omega(1)}, no STPP construction in this family can prove omega = 2. This reduces the question of ruling out omega = 2 via STPP to establishing sublinear slice rank upper bounds on the group multiplication tensor.
