# arXiv 1009.5526

Title: Commutativity Degree, Its Generalizations, and Classification of Finite Groups
Year: 2010

### Nath-Das Theorem 2.4.1 (Lower bound for Pr(G) via |G'| and |G:Z(G)|)

For any finite group G, Pr(G) >= 1/|G'| * (1 + (|G'| - 1)/|G:Z(G)|). In particular Pr(G) > 1/|G'| for nonabelian G. This lower bound connects the commutativity degree to the commutator subgroup size and central index, both of which are invariants computed by the group sieve. Formalizing this gives computable lower bounds on Pr(G) from the sieve's metadata.

### Nath-Das Theorem 2.4.3 (Characterization of groups attaining the Pr(G) lower bound)

For a finite nonabelian group G, the lower bound is attained iff cd(G) = {1, |G:Z(G)|^{1/2}}, which means G is of central type with |cd(G)| = 2. The paper gives six equivalent conditions including: (a) |Cl_G(x)| = |G'| for all x in G - Z(G), (b) Cl_G(x) = G'x for all x in G - Z(G) (which implies G is nilpotent of class 2), (c) C_G(x) is normal in G and G' is isomorphic to G/C_G(x) for all x in G - Z(G) (which implies G is a CN-group), and (d) G' = {[y,x] : y in G} for all x in G - Z(G) (so every element of G' is a commutator). These characterizations link Pr(G) extremality to the character degree set cd(G), a key sieve invariant.

### Nath-Das Proposition 2.4.7 (Isoclinism and commutativity degree)

Groups isoclinic to extraspecial p-groups are characterized as nonabelian groups with |G'| = p and G' <= Z(G). Since isoclinism preserves Pr(G), this reduces the commutativity degree computation to a canonical representative. Relevant because the sieve classifies p-groups by structural invariants that are isoclinism-invariant.

### Nath-Das Theorem 2.3.3 (Classification of odd-order groups with Pr(G) >= 11/75)

Complete classification of all finite groups of odd order with Pr(G) >= 11/75, giving the exact values of Pr(G) and the structure of G', G' cap Z(G), and G/Z(G) in each case. This is a discrete characterization theorem that can be formalized as a finite case analysis over the table of groups.
