# arXiv 1406.5145

Title: Geometric lower bounds for generalized ranks
Year: 2014

### Geometric lower bounds for generalized ranks

Geometric methods (via secant varieties) for proving lower bounds on various notions of rank (Waring rank, rank with respect to arbitrary varieties, multihomogeneous ranks). Improves catalecticant lower bounds by adding dimensions of singularity loci, generalizing results of Landsberg--Teitler and Ranestad--Schreyer to multihomogeneous polynomials and ranks with respect to arbitrary varieties. Includes an example computing bounds for the matrix multiplication tensor rank.
