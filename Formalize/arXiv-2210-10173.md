# arXiv 2210.10173

Title: Faster Matrix Multiplication via Asymmetric Hashing
Year: 2022

### Duan-Wu-Zhou (omega < 2.371866)

Improved omega bound via asymmetric hashing within the laser method framework. The paper uses the laser method applied to powers of the Coppersmith-Winograd tensor, not the group-theoretic approach of Cohn-Umans.
